An Image That Invents Its Outside

Begin with one image, then reveal the larger world around it, three times. Each reveal keeps what came before and changes what it means.

Make each expansion in your own image tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, Photoshop's Generative Expand…). This page prepares what to give it and plays the reveals. Your images are saved in this folder (public/my-images) and go nowhere else.
