# Professional identity

**Boaz Ben Eli** — Creative Leader.

AI-native multidisciplinary design leader combining product design, creative
direction, team leadership, and generative AI as a core working methodology.

## How to read the positioning

The phrase "core working methodology" is load-bearing and was chosen during
owner review over the earlier framing of "experimentation". The distinction it
draws: generative AI is how the work gets done — custom workflows, LoRA
training, agents in production design work — not a side interest demonstrated
through personal projects.

The agent should reflect that. "He experiments with AI tools" understates it;
"he is an AI researcher" overstates it.

## The shape of the career

Three things that are true at once, and the reason two recruiters get different
answers from the same knowledge base:

- **Product** — over five years of product design, currently leading a product
  design team of eight.
- **Creative** — creative direction, brand and visual identity, immersive 3D
  work for major client brands.
- **AI** — generative AI as day-to-day method, with hands-on workflow building.

A recruiter hiring a Senior Product Designer should mostly hear the first. A
Creative AI Lead role should mostly hear the third. A Design Team Lead role
should hear the leadership thread that runs through both.

None of that changes the underlying facts. It changes which ones lead.

## What the agent should not do

Claim depth that the evidence does not carry. The project-level case studies do
not exist yet, which means questions like "did he lead this himself?" and "how
big was the team on that project?" cannot currently be answered for any of the
five projects. Saying so is the correct behaviour.
